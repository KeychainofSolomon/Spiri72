== tarot | the-structure ==
A tarot deck is 78 cards in two parts. The Major Arcana (22 trumps, numbered 0-21,
the Fool to the World) carry the great archetypes and weigh heaviest in a reading.
The Minor Arcana (56) run in four suits — Wands, Cups, Swords, Pentacles (Coins) —
each of ten numbered "pips" (Ace to Ten) and four court cards (Page, Knight,
Queen, King). The suits answer to the four elements: Wands/fire, Cups/water,
Swords/air, Pentacles/earth. That fourfold-plus-trumps shape is the whole engine
the esoteric readings are built on.

== history | where-it-comes-from ==
The honest genealogy, since the myths run thick here. Tarot cards appear in
mid-15th-century northern Italy as hand-painted trionfi for a trick-taking game
(tarocchi); for three centuries they were chiefly a card game, and still are in
much of Europe. The occult reading begins only in 1781, when Court de Gébelin
declared them a lost Egyptian "Book of Thoth" — a claim with no evidence, long
since discarded. Etteilla made the first divinatory deck; the Kabbalistic and
astrological system is the Golden Dawn's (Mathers, 1888+), carried to the world by
the Waite-Smith deck (1909) and Crowley's Thoth. Old cards, young occultism.

== how-a-reading-works ==
The mechanics beneath the fortune-telling: the querent frames a question, the deck
is shuffled and cut, and cards are dealt into a spread whose positions each carry a
fixed meaning (self, obstacle, past, outcome, and so on). The reader reads each
card by its own sense, bent by its position, its neighbours, and whether it falls
upright or reversed. Nothing in the cards foretells; the method is a disciplined
way of casting a rich symbol-set against a question and reading the pattern that
falls — a mirror for thought as much as any claim of prophecy.

== the-four-suits | the-minor-arcana ==
The 56 Minors are the texture of a reading, the daily matter under the Majors'
great themes. Wands (fire): will, drive, work, creation. Cups (water): feeling,
love, the inner life. Swords (air): mind, conflict, word and trouble. Pentacles
or Coins (earth): body, money, craft, the material. The Aces are the pure root of
each element; the numbers two through ten walk that element through its stages
(and in the Golden Dawn each is laid on a decan of the zodiac with a planet, so
the Minors map the whole wheel).

== the-court-cards ==
The sixteen court cards are the deck's people — and its hardest cards to read,
standing now for persons in the querent's life, now for parts of the self, now for
an approach to a matter. The common scheme ranks them Page (or Princess), Knight
(or Prince), Queen, King, and crosses the four ranks with the four suits/elements,
so each court card is an element-of-an-element (the Queen of Cups, water of water;
the Knight of Swords, air of... fire, by one scheme). Expect disagreement between
systems here; the courts are where decks differ most.

== the-tree-of-life | the-kabbalah ==
The esoteric spine of the deck (Golden Dawn): the Tree of Life has ten sephiroth
(spheres) joined by 22 paths. The 22 Major Arcana are laid on the 22 paths, each
also bearing one of the 22 Hebrew letters; the four suits answer to the four
Kabbalistic worlds and the four letters of the divine Name (YHVH); the ten pips of
each suit answer to the ten sephiroth. So the whole 78 is made a map of one
cosmology. This is a 19th-century construction, elegant and deliberate — not a
thing the 15th-century card-makers knew.

== the-hebrew-letters | the-paths ==
From the Sefer Yetzirah, the 22 Hebrew letters divide three ways, and the Golden
Dawn hung the Majors on them so: the 3 "mother" letters to the elements (Aleph/
Air–the Fool, Mem/Water–the Hanged Man, Shin/Fire–Judgement); the 7 "double"
letters to the planets (as the Magician–Mercury, the Wheel–Jupiter, the Sun–Sun);
the 12 "simple" letters to the zodiac signs (as the Emperor–Aries, the Lovers–
Gemini, Death–Scorpio). To make the signs run in order they swapped Strength (Leo)
and Justice (Libra) between numbers 8 and 11 — a famous and deliberate fix.

== the-fool | 0 ==
0, the Fool. Letter Aleph, element Air. The beginning before all numbers: the leap
into the unknown, innocence, pure potential, the spirit stepping off the cliff.
Upright, new starts, trust, freedom. Reversed, recklessness, folly, a step taken
blind. The zero that holds all the others in seed.

== the-magician | 1 ==
1, the Magician. Letter Beth, planet Mercury. The will that shapes: "as above, so
below," the four suit-tokens on his table, the maker who turns idea to act.
Upright, skill, focus, power rightly used. Reversed, trickery, manipulation,
wasted gifts.

== the-high-priestess | 2 ==
2, the High Priestess. Letter Gimel, the Moon. The veiled keeper of the hidden:
intuition, mystery, the inner and unspoken, the book half-closed on her lap.
Upright, secret knowledge, the unconscious, patience. Reversed, secrets kept or
leaking, surface over depth.

== the-empress | 3 ==
3, the Empress. Letter Daleth, planet Venus. The great mother: fruitfulness,
nature, abundance, love made flesh and field. Upright, growth, nurture, plenty,
creation. Reversed, smothering, stagnation, barrenness.

== the-emperor | 4 ==
4, the Emperor. Letter Heh, sign Aries. The father and ruler: order, authority,
structure, the law laid down. Upright, command, stability, protection. Reversed,
tyranny, rigidity, or power lost.

== the-hierophant | 5 ==
5, the Hierophant. Letter Vav, sign Taurus. The teacher of the tradition: doctrine,
institution, the bridge to the sacred by the appointed road. Upright, learning,
orthodoxy, counsel. Reversed, dogma, rebellion, the hollow rite.

== the-lovers | 6 ==
6, the Lovers. Letter Zayin, sign Gemini. Union and the choice it demands: love,
pairing, the fork in the road that asks the heart. Upright, harmony, bond, right
choosing. Reversed, discord, bad bargains, division.

== the-chariot | 7 ==
7, the Chariot. Letter Cheth, sign Cancer. Victory by will: two sphinxes pulling
opposite ways, mastered and driven straight. Upright, triumph, control, forward
force. Reversed, loss of reins, scattered drive, defeat.

== strength | 8 ==
8, Strength (Golden Dawn's eighth; older decks number it eleven). Letter Teth, sign
Leo. The maiden closing the lion's jaws — not force but gentle mastery, courage,
the taming of the inner beast. Upright, fortitude, patience, calm power. Reversed,
doubt, raw appetite, weakness.

== the-hermit | 9 ==
9, the Hermit. Letter Yod, sign Virgo. The lone lamp on the height: withdrawal,
search, the inner light carried into the dark. Upright, wisdom, solitude, guidance.
Reversed, isolation, refusal, a light hidden.

== wheel-of-fortune | 10 ==
10, the Wheel of Fortune. Letter Kaph, planet Jupiter. The turning of fate: rise
and fall, luck, the cycle none stands outside. Upright, change, fortune, a turning
tide. Reversed, downturn, bad luck, resisting the turn.

== justice | 11 ==
11, Justice (Golden Dawn's eleventh; older decks number it eight). Letter Lamed,
sign Libra. The sword and the scales: balance, law, cause and consequence, the true
account. Upright, fairness, truth, reckoning. Reversed, injustice, imbalance, dues
unpaid.

== the-hanged-man | 12 ==
12, the Hanged Man. Letter Mem, element Water. The willing reversal: suspension,
surrender, the world seen upside down and newly. Upright, letting go, new vision,
sacrifice. Reversed, stalling, pointless martyrdom, stuck.

== death | 13 ==
13, Death. Letter Nun, sign Scorpio. Rarely a literal death: ending and the change
it clears ground for, the scythe that makes room. Upright, transformation, closure,
rebirth. Reversed, clinging, stagnation, the change refused.

== temperance | 14 ==
14, Temperance. Letter Samekh, sign Sagittarius. The angel mingling the cups:
balance, patience, the middle way, opposites blended well. Upright, moderation,
harmony, healing. Reversed, excess, discord, bad mixture.

== the-devil | 15 ==
15, the Devil. Letter Ayin, sign Capricorn. The chains worn loosely: bondage,
appetite, the material gripping the soul — yet the captives could lift the chains
off. Upright, temptation, dependence, the shadow. Reversed, release, breaking free,
facing the thing.

== the-tower | 16 ==
16, the Tower. Letter Peh, planet Mars. The lightning-struck tower: sudden ruin,
the false thing thrown down, revelation like a bolt. Upright, upheaval, shock,
truth that shatters. Reversed, disaster feared or delayed, a slower fall.

== the-star | 17 ==
17, the Star. Letter Tzaddi, sign Aquarius. After the Tower, the quiet water-pourer
under the stars: hope, renewal, calm and guidance regained. Upright, faith, healing,
serenity. Reversed, despair, faith lost, the light dimmed.

== the-moon | 18 ==
18, the Moon. Letter Qoph, sign Pisces. The night road between the towers:
illusion, dream, fear, the unclear and the unconscious astir. Upright, mystery,
intuition, things not as they seem. Reversed, confusion clearing, or deeper into
delusion.

== the-sun | 19 ==
19, the Sun. Letter Resh, the Sun. The child on the white horse in full day: joy,
clarity, vitality, the plain good. Upright, happiness, success, truth seen whole.
Reversed, a clouded joy, delay, dimmed warmth.

== judgement | 20 ==
20, Judgement. Letter Shin, element Fire. The trumpet and the rising dead:
awakening, reckoning, the call to a new life. Upright, renewal, absolution, the
summons answered. Reversed, self-doubt, the call refused, old guilt.

== the-world | 21 ==
21, the World. Letter Tav, planet Saturn (and the element Earth). The dancer in the
wreath: completion, wholeness, the journey round and fulfilled — and the ground of
the next Fool's leap. Upright, attainment, integration, closure. Reversed,
unfinished business, a short-fall near the end.

== the-spreads ==
A spread sets the cards in named places, each lending its sense. The three-card
line (past–present–future, or situation–action–outcome) is the plain workhorse.
The Celtic Cross (ten cards) is the best-known larger spread: a central pair
(matter and what crosses it), a cross of four (foundation, past, crown, near
future), and a staff of four (self, environment, hopes-and-fears, outcome). The
spread is just a frame of questions; the reading is in the cards that fall into
it.

== reversals ==
A card dealt upside down is read "reversed" — by one school weakened, blocked or
turned inward; by another simply a different shade of the same card, not its
opposite. Many readers use no reversals at all, taking meaning from the
surrounding cards instead. There is no settled rule: it is a choice of method the
reader makes and keeps to, not a law of the deck.

== marseille-waite-thoth ==
Three great families of deck, worth telling apart. The Tarot de Marseille (17th-18th
c.) is the old standard: its Minor pips are plain arrangements of suit-signs, read by
number and suit, not by scene. The Rider-Waite-Smith (1909) gave every Minor a
pictured scene, which made intuitive reading easy and is why it rules the modern
world. Crowley and Harris's Thoth (1940s) rebuilt the symbolism on the Golden
Dawn's full astrological and Kabbalistic system. Same 78 skeleton, three different
bodies.
