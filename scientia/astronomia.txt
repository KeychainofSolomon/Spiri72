# nomen: the book of the spheres

== saturn | saturnus | cronos ==
Saturn, the highest and slowest of the seven wandering stars, cold and dry,
greatest in reach and least in haste. Reckoned the greater malefic: of lead,
of age, of boundaries, bone and stone, of long patience and of melancholy. Its
day is Saturday, its metal lead, its course near thirty years through the twelve
signs. Rules Capricorn and Aquarius in the old scheme.

== jupiter | iuppiter | jove | zeus ==
Jupiter, beneath Saturn, temperate and warm, the greater benefic. Of tin, of
law and increase, of mercy, princes and right religion. Its day is Thursday, its
metal tin, its course near twelve years. Rules Sagittarius and Pisces.

== mars | mars-planet | ares ==
Mars, hot and dry, the lesser malefic: of iron, of war, fire, fever, the knife
and sudden anger. Red in the sky. Its day is Tuesday, its metal iron, its course
near two years. Rules Aries and Scorpio.

== sun | sol | helios ==
The Sun, the heart of the seven, hot and dry yet life-giving, the fount of day.
Of gold, of kings, the heart, honour and the single rule. Its day is Sunday, its
metal gold, its course the year through the zodiac, the measure of all the rest.
Rules Leo.

== venus | venus-planet | aphrodite ==
Venus, temperate and moist, the lesser benefic: of copper, of love, music,
concord, pleasure and fair things. Seen as morning and evening star. Its day is
Friday, its metal copper. Rules Taurus and Libra.

== mercury | mercurius | hermes ==
Mercury, swift and changeable, taking the nature of what it joins: of
quicksilver, of speech, trade, letters, cunning and the wit. Never far from the
Sun. Its day is Wednesday, its metal quicksilver. Rules Gemini and Virgo.

== moon | luna | selene ==
The Moon, nearest and swiftest, cold and moist, ruler of tides, waters, growth
and the common folk. Her light is borrowed from the Sun; she waxes and wanes
through near thirty days and is reckoned by her age in the choosing of hours. Of
silver. Her day is Monday. Rules Cancer.

== zodiac | zodiacus | the-twelve-signs ==
The zodiac, the belt of twelve signs through which the Sun and wandering stars
pass: Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius,
Capricorn, Aquarius, Pisces. Four of fire, four of earth, four of air, four of
water; each the house of one or more of the seven, and each ruling a part of the
body from head (Aries) to feet (Pisces).
