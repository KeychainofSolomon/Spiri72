# Traced seals of the 72 spirits
Source: plates of the 1916 de Laurence printing of the Mathers/Crowley *Goetia* (Project Gutenberg 72679, `front-3-large.jpg` figs 1–24, `front-4-large.jpg` figs 25–68 in alternate rows, `front-5-large.jpg` figs 29–72 in alternate rows, `front-6-large.jpg` figs 73–80), public domain. Traced with `tools/sigils/extract.py` (OpenCV contours, the `trace.py` method); the match column is the overlap of the tracing with the plate's ink (intersection over union).
Doubled seals: Bune takes fig. 31 by the owner's ruling ("the least is best", Peterson quoting Sloane 3648 and 2731); for the other seven the first figure is used and the second kept as `-variant`, an open decision.
Sloane 3825 (the oldest seal witness found) has not yet been compared seal by seal.
